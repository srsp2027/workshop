WORKSHOP · Geometric Measure Theory & Calculus of Variations

Structure and Rigidity of Sets, Measures and PDEs

17–21 May 2027 · Centro De Giorgi, Pisa

ERC ConFine N.101078057
MSCA DireStratS N.101275806

01

Description

Our workshop aims to bring together established and early-career researchers in geometric measure theory and the calculus of variations.

Topics include rectifiability, the structure of measures under differential constraints, fine properties of functions, rigidity and regularity for sets, measures and PDEs.

The programme features a dedicated poster session. Funding is available to support early career scientists — see Registration.
